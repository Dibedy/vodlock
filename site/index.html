How does SPOILLESS avoid spoilers?

It keeps scores, video length, thumbnails, and the provider timeline out of the way, so you can choose what to reveal as you watch.

Can I jump to the next round?

Yes. Every indexed broadcast has round and map navigation, with controls for moving past pauses, replays, and timeouts.

Which broadcasts are available?

SPOILLESS indexes selected official VCT YouTube broadcasts and supported Twitch watch parties when a reliable round index is available.
