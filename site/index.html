Good to know

When will a new match be up?

I'm aiming for within about 2 hours after the match ends. It's not a fixed deadline though. Some matches are ready sooner, and some take longer.

What's being processed?

My indexer finds and checks the round starts before the VOD goes up. I don't have to wait for the whole stream to end. Once a match is ready, I can publish it, with final checks done later.

What about watch parties and chat?

I sync watch parties to the official match. Pauses, replays or the streamer switching away can slow that down. The official VOD doesn't wait for the watch party. Chat can be added separately once the timing is checked.

Why isn't a match there yet?

The recording might not be ready, or something needs another check. I'd rather retry it or take another look than give you bad timestamps. I don't cover every broadcast yet.

What stays hidden?

I keep match results, video length and the original player timeline out of the way. You can skip between rounds and maps without seeing how much is left. Settings lets you choose which details to show.
