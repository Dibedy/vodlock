Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to data when you use SPOILLESS.

What SPOILLESS collects

SPOILLESS does not provide user accounts, forms, comments, payments, newsletters, advertising, or first-party analytics. Viewing preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. When you visit, Vercel may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser information, requested page, and request time to deliver and secure the site. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Video playback

SPOILLESS uses the YouTube API Services and privacy-enhanced YouTube player for YouTube videos, and the official Twitch embedded player for Twitch videos. A provider's player and script are not loaded until you select Start watching. After you start, that provider may receive technical and playback data under its own policies. See the YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy, Twitch Terms of Service, and Twitch Privacy Notice.

Round indexes

SPOILLESS downloads a precomputed index containing a public YouTube or Twitch video identifier and round start times. The index does not contain viewer data.

Archived VOD chat

For selected Twitch broadcasts, SPOILLESS stores public chat messages, display names, colors, emote identifiers, and their positions in the broadcast. Chat is read-only and synchronized to playback. Twitch emote images are loaded from Twitch's content delivery network.
