Privacy

What the site saves, and which services your browser connects to.

On your device

You don't need a SPOILLESS account. Settings, favourite teams, watched matches, keyboard shortcuts and your last playback position are saved in your browser's local storage. Clear the site's stored data in your browser to remove them. There are no newsletters or ads.

Visits and analytics

Vercel Web Analytics helps us see overall visits and page views. It uses no analytics cookies and doesn't track you across websites. Its statistics can include the page, referrer, approximate location, browser and device type. You can read more in Vercel's analytics privacy details.

Hosting

Vercel hosts the site. To serve pages and keep the site secure, it may process your IP address, browser details, requested page and request time. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Watching videos

YouTube videos use the YouTube API Services and privacy-enhanced player. Twitch videos use Twitch's official player. Their player and script load only after you press Start watching. From then on, the provider may receive technical and playback data, and may use cookies under its own policies. See Google's privacy policy, YouTube's terms, Twitch's privacy notice and Twitch's terms.

Round timestamps

The site loads a prepared list of round start times for each video. That list contains a public video ID and timestamps, not viewer data.

Archived chat and emotes

For some broadcasts, we save public Twitch chat messages, display names, colours, emote IDs and when each message was sent in the stream. They're replayed alongside the video, not sent as new chat messages.

Opening a VOD with chat can also connect your browser to 7TV to look up emotes. Emote images come from Twitch and 7TV. These requests can share your IP address and browser details with those services, even before you start the video. See 7TV's privacy policy and Twitch's privacy notice.

Supporting the site

The support link takes you to Ko-fi. Donations are handled there, under Ko-fi's policies. SPOILLESS doesn't process or store your payment details.

Questions?

Message @dibedy on Discord for privacy questions or a request about an archived chat message.
