Using the site

SPOILLESS adds round and map navigation to selected public YouTube and Twitch broadcasts. The original videos stay on those platforms. We don't host or redistribute them.

Watching here also means agreeing to the relevant YouTube terms or Twitch terms. Those platforms and the content owners decide whether a video is available and how it plays.

Use it fairly

The site is for lawful, personal viewing. Don't disrupt it, bypass its security, misuse the round-index data or use it to infringe someone else's rights.

Things can change

Round timestamps can be wrong, especially if a recording is edited or removed. Broadcasts may be added, changed or taken down, and the site may go offline without notice. Processing times are estimates, not deadlines.

Videos, team and event names, logos and other third-party material belong to their owners. SPOILLESS doesn't claim ownership of them.

Archived chat

Some VODs include a read-only replay of public Twitch chat. The messages are written by viewers, who remain responsible for what they post. Chat may be incomplete or unavailable.

Optional support
